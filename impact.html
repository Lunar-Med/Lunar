Impact & philanthropy

Healthcare access is part of our business model.

Lunar Medical was founded with a dual mission: deliver high-quality, affordable medical products, and use what we build to reach patients and communities that are too often left behind.

Maternal health

Prenatal care for families in need.

In summer 2026 we supplied prenatal vitamins for a maternal health shipment to Cuba organized with the Pan-American Medical Association.

We sourced and shipped more than 200 bottles of prenatal multivitamins with folic acid and DHA directly to the association's warehouse, so expectant mothers could receive the nutrition that supports a healthy pregnancy.

Surgical access

Affordable sutures, where they are needed most.

We are developing a surgical suture program that pairs affordable U.S. supply with donations to clinics and medical missions in underserved regions of Latin America.

  • Non-absorbable monofilament sutures for clinics and surgery centers
  • Revenue from U.S. sales helps fund missions, logistics and training
  • Transparent impact reporting for donors, partners and investors
