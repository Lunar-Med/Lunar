About Lunar Medical

A physician-led company bridging global manufacturing and local care.

Lunar Medical exists so that essential medical supplies are high quality, fairly priced, fully documented and able to reach the people who need them.

Our story

Built on medicine, sourcing and service.

Lunar Medical is the medical business of Lunar LLC, a Washington State company. We partner with ISO 13485 certified manufacturers in China and manage the full program for buyers in North America: product selection, compliance, packaging, pricing and delivery.

Our work spans clinical consumables, retail private label and home health products. Alongside it, we supply and donate to health organizations serving underserved communities, because access to care is the reason this work matters.

Mission

Accessible, affordable, ethically sourced.

To source, manufacture and distribute high-quality medical products that are accessible, affordable and ethically sourced, supporting both clinical excellence and humanitarian outreach.

Vision

A leading name in trusted medical supply.

To become a leading provider of FDA-compliant medical products in the United States while funding global access to care through reinvestment in health organizations.
