Solutions

Medical sourcing that is transparent, compliant and built to scale.

We run sourcing programs end to end for hospitals, distributors, national retailers and health organizations, with certified manufacturing partners and a team that answers within one business day.

How we source

From first sample to final delivery.

A clear, four-stage process keeps every program on spec, on budget and on time.

Source & qualify

We match your requirements to certified factories, compare quotes and deliver retail-ready samples for testing.

Verify compliance

We assemble the full file: ISO 13485 certificates, audit reports, FDA registration and FEI numbers for every product.

Produce & inspect

Production runs to the approved sample and specification, with a certificate of analysis for every lot.

Deliver & support

We coordinate purchase orders, electronic ordering, freight and invoicing, and stay with you after delivery.

Quality & compliance

Standards our partners sign up to.

Every manufacturing partner commits to a written set of expectations before a single unit ships.

  • Compliance owner. FDA registration and listings kept current for every factory, renewed each year.
  • Factory transparency. The plant, address and FEI named for each product. No unannounced subcontractors.
  • Quality. Product matches the approved sample, with a COA per lot and an active CAPA process.
  • Firm pricing. FOB quotes held for an agreed period, with open cost breakdowns.
  • Responsiveness. Replies within one business day; compliance changes reported within five.
