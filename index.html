Quality you can verify

Compliance is where we start, not where we finish.

Every product we place is made in facilities that meet the standards hospitals and national retailers require. We hold our manufacturing partners to them in writing.

  • Manufacturing partners certified to ISO 13485, the quality standard FDA now recognizes for medical devices
  • FDA establishment registration and listing kept current for every factory
  • Factory transparency: the named plant and FDA FEI number for every product
  • Certificate of analysis per lot and an active corrective-action (CAPA) process
  • Independent checks for FDA import alerts and warning letters before anything ships
