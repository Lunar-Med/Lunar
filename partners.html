How partnership works

Clear roles. Shared wins.

  • One channel, regular rhythm. Weekly calls during setup, a shared tracker for documents, samples and orders, and named backups on both sides.
  • Win together on price. Open cost breakdowns so we can meet target price points without cutting quality.
  • A shared pipeline. We grow with partners into new categories, from diagnostics to wellness.
  • Put it in writing. Partnership terms, defined roles and protection for the relationships each side brings.
